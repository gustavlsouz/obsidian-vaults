Perfeito, aqui vai a lista completa das **principais transformações de tempos verbais no reported speech**, também chamadas de **"backshifting"**. Essas “voltas no passado” são típicas quando o verbo introdutório está no passado (como _said_, _told_, _asked_).

---

## 🔁 Tabela de Transformação dos Tempos Verbais no Reported Speech

| **Discurso Direto (tempo original)** | **Reported Speech (tempo após a "volta")**  | **Exemplo direto**              | **Exemplo indireto**                         |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| **Simple Present**                   | Simple Past                                 | "I use Docker."                 | He said he **used** Docker.                  |
| **Present Continuous**               | Past Continuous                             | "I am learning Go."             | She said she **was learning** Go.            |
| **Present Perfect**                  | Past Perfect                                | "I have fixed the bug."         | He said he **had fixed** the bug.            |
| **Present Perfect Continuous**       | Past Perfect Continuous                     | "I have been coding."           | She said she **had been coding**.            |
| **Simple Past**                      | Past Perfect                                | "I deployed the app."           | He said he **had deployed** the app.         |
| **Past Continuous**                  | Past Perfect Continuous                     | "I was debugging."              | She said she **had been debugging**.         |
| **Past Perfect**                     | (permanece igual – **não muda**)            | "I had finished the script."    | He said he **had finished** the script.      |
| **Past Perfect Continuous**          | (permanece igual – **não muda**)            | "I had been testing."           | She said she **had been testing**.           |
| **Will**                             | **Would**                                   | "I will deploy soon."           | He said he **would deploy** soon.            |
| **Can**                              | **Could**                                   | "I can help."                   | She said she **could help**.                 |
| **May**                              | **Might**                                   | "I may refactor this."          | He said he **might refactor** that.          |
| **Shall**                            | **Should** ou **would** (dependendo do uso) | "I shall present the solution." | She said she **would present** the solution. |
| **Must**                             | **Had to** (geralmente)                     | "I must deploy today."          | He said he **had to deploy** that day.       |

---

### 📌 Observações importantes:

- **Se o verbo introdutório está no presente (ex: _says_, _tells_), normalmente não há mudança de tempo verbal.**
    
    - _She says, “I use Linux.”_ → _She says she uses Linux._
        
- **Algumas exceções de "no change"**:
    
    - Quando a situação ainda é **verdadeira no momento da fala**.
        
        - _“I live in Canada.”_ → He said he **lives** in Canada. ✅ (ainda mora lá)
